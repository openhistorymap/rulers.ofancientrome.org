# Design context — rulers.ofancientrome.org

A gallery of the rulers of Rome, from Romulus to the fall of the West in 476,
where you walk the line of kings, consuls, and emperors — and, for those whose
lives are well enough recorded, speak with an avatar of them.

Sibling in spirit to the OHM map atlases (`map.ofww1.org`), but this is **not a
map** — it is a **portrait gallery on a spine of time**. The hero is the face
and the line of succession, not a basemap.

## Aesthetic

**Inscriptional, lapidary, marble.** The feeling of a museum hall of imperial
busts: travertine walls, gilt labels, purple porphyry. Restrained, confident,
unhurried. Never "toga-party kitsch" — no faux-Latin gimmicks, no laurel-wreath
clip-art. Dignity over decoration.

- **Display type**: `Cinzel` — drawn from Roman square capitals / the Trajan
  inscription. Used for names, the wordmark, section headers. SMALL CAPS energy.
- **Body type**: `EB Garamond` — humanist serif, warm and readable for the
  biographies pulled from Wikipedia.
- **Palette** — warm stone, not cold grey:
  - Travertine ground `#f4efe6`, paper `#faf6ee`, ink `#2a2320`.
  - **Period accents** carry meaning (used for chips, rails, the timeline bands):
    - Kingdom → terracotta bronze `#a8602f`
    - Republic → senatorial crimson `#8c2f39`
    - Empire → Tyrian/imperial purple `#6b2c5f`
  - Gilt `#b08d4f` for rules, hairlines, the gold-leaf detail.
- **Dark theme** ("lamplit gallery"): ink ground `#181310`, marble text
  `#e9e0d2`, the three period accents brightened. Toggle persists in
  localStorage; respects `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Layout

- **Topbar**: wordmark (Cinzel), one-line tagline, search, theme toggle.
- **Left margin**: period filters (Kingdom · Republic · Empire), a dynasty list
  that scroll-jumps, live counts, and the "speak with them" legend.
- **Timeline ribbon**: a slim horizontal scale, 753 BC → AD 476, with the three
  period bands at true proportion. Click a band to filter + scroll. It is
  navigation and orientation, not a data viz — keep it quiet.
- **Roster**: the canonical infoplease order, grouped under period → dynasty
  headers. Each ruler is a **card**: bust thumbnail, name in Cinzel, regnal or
  life years, a one-line gloss, a dynasty chip, and — when the figure is
  well-documented — a "speak with them" button.
- **Detail drawer** (right): large bust, full biography, dates, dynasty,
  **succession** (← predecessor · successor →), birthplace, titles held, and
  outbound links (Wikipedia · Wikidata · infoplease). The avatar lives here.
- **Handoff**: after the last ruler (476), a porphyry card — *"The Western
  Empire falls. The story continues."* — linking to **rulers.ofthepast.org**,
  the sibling site that carries the same system forward through the centuries
  after Rome.

## The avatar

A "speak with them" affordance on well-documented rulers. The chat is **grounded
only in that ruler's harvested facts** — the Wikipedia lead and the Wikidata
dates/relations — so the persona stays factual rather than inventing history.
For now the shell is real (an in-character opening, the framing, the input) and
the model wiring is deferred; the opener is templated client-side from the
harvested bio so it already feels alive. Tone: first person, measured, a little
imperious — but it must defer to the record and never fabricate.

## House rules

- No build step. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, MapLibre-free. Relative paths only so the
  custom domain and any `/staging/` subpath both work.
- `data/` is machine-owned (harvester output). Never hand-edit it.
- Accessibility: real focus states, `aria` on the drawer and toggles, keyboard
  closable, alt text on busts (the ruler's name).
- Images are hot-linked from Wikimedia Commons / Wikipedia at harvest-chosen
  sizes; carry the CC/credit in the detail panel's source line.
